The integrity of digital forensic evidence—such as
DNA profiles, toxicology reports, and pathology findings—is
critical in legal and medical domains. This project presents
a blockchain-based system for verifying the authenticity of
such digital files using SHA-256 hashing and Ethereum smart
contracts. By storing hash values on a tamper-proof ledger,
the system enables real-time validation of uploaded evidence
without revealing its contents. A mismatch in hashes indicates
potential tampering, thereby safeguarding evidence integrity and
enhancing trust in digital forensic processes.
It eliminates the need for a central authority and provides
a user-friendly interface for law enforcement, forensic experts,
and medical professionals to verify the integrity of critical digital
evidence in real-time.
